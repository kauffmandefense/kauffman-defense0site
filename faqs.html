Frequently Asked Questions

CPL Training Courses

Q: What is included in the CPL training course?

A: Our CPL course includes classroom instruction on firearm safety, Michigan firearm laws, self-defense laws, and live-fire range training. You’ll also receive an official MCRGO certificate upon successful completion, which you can use to apply for your Michigan CPL.

Q: How long does the CPL class last?

A: All of our public classes begin at 8 a.m. and run until approximately 4 p.m., covering both classroom instruction and live-fire range requirements.

Q: Is this class required to get my CPL in Michigan?

A: Yes, Michigan law requires applicants to complete an approved Concealed Pistol License (CPL) training course prior to submitting their application with the county clerk.

Q: Who can take this class?

A: You must be at least 18 years old to attend the class. However, to officially apply for a Michigan CPL, you must be at least 21 years old and satisfy state legal criteria.

Q: Do I need prior firearms experience to take this class?

A: No, our course is designed for all experience levels. We cover the foundational elements of firearm safety, handling mechanics, and marksmanship techniques from the ground up.

Q: What do I need to bring to the class?

A: Students should bring a handgun, 100 rounds of factory ammunition, ear and eye protection, and a notebook with pen. For a full detailed list, visit our What to Bring page.

Q: What if I don’t own a firearm?

A: If you do not own a firearm, we offer a .22 caliber pistol rental for an additional fee of $20, which includes the firearm and required ammunition for the range portion.

Q: How much does the class cost?

A: The standard CPL class fee is $100.

Q: Do you offer any discounts?

A: Yes, we offer a $10 discount to active military, veterans, National Guard, law enforcement, corrections officers, dispatchers, EMS personnel, teachers, and hospital employees.

Q: Do you offer private classes?

A: Yes! We offer private classes at our facility or at your preferred location (minimum 4 students). Contact us via our Contact Form to arrange dates.

Q: Is lunch provided during CPL classes?

A: Yes! For standard classes held at our location, we provide sandwiches, chips, water, and coffee. (Note: Lunch is not provided for off-site private courses).

Q: How do I apply for my Michigan CPL after completing the class?

A: Take your completed training certificate to your local county clerk’s office, submit your application, complete fingerprinting, and pay the state application fee.

FFL Transfers, Sales & Gunsmithing

Q: How do incoming FFL transfers work?

A: If you purchase a firearm online or from another licensed dealer, select Kauffman Defense, LLC as your receiving transfer dealer. Once your item arrives and is logged into our inventory, we will contact you to schedule an appointment for background processing and pickup.

Q: What is your FFL transfer fee?

A: Our standard transfer fee is $25 per firearm.

Q: What identification do I need to bring when picking up a firearm?

A: You must present a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (such as a Michigan Driver's License) showing your current physical residential address. If your driver's license displays a past address, secondary official government documentation (e.g., vehicle registration) is required.

Q: Do you accept store walk-ins or are services by appointment?

A: To provide dedicated attention for each client, all firearm transfers, inventory inquiries, and gunsmithing consultations are conducted by appointment. Please submit an inquiry through our Contact Form to schedule a time.

Q: How do I request gunsmithing or repair services?

A: You can reach out via our contact page detailing your firearm make, model, and the service requested (e.g., cleaning, inspection, part installation, sight mounting, or custom assembly). We will provide a initial quote and schedule an intake appointment.

Q: Can I reserve an item from your online inventory?

A: Yes! Browse our Inventory Page and click "Inquire / Reserve" on any item. This will automatically open our contact form with the product details so we can assist you with your purchase.
